About LUBBU

Specialist care, founded by specialists.

LUBBU is a multi-specialty medical center that brings maternal & child health together with internal medicine under one roof. Founded by experienced specialist doctors, we deliver tertiary-level outpatient and inpatient care — at an affordable cost, with innovative packages and discounts.

  • State-of-the-art diagnostic & treatment equipment
  • Two fully equipped operating theatres — one dedicated to C-sections
  • Painless delivery, Neonatal ICU and chemotherapy services
  • A safe, clean and modern environment
Vision

To be the first choice for excellent health care services.

Mission

Improving the health and well-being of individuals, families and our community through innovation and pursuit of excellence.
